## Controls

### Movement
- **W A S D** - move
- **Mouse** - look around
- **Shift** (hold) - sprint (only works while standing)
- **Shift** while crouched - stand back up
- **Space** - jump
- **Space x2 fast** (while holding Hand + facing a wall) - wall climb

### Weapons
- **1** - switch to Gun
- **2** - switch to HT Radio
- **3** - switch to Hand
- **Left Click** - shoot (Gun) / turn HT Radio on-off + on mic
- **Right Click** - raise/lower weapon (ready stance), needed before you can shoot
- **R** - reload
- **V** - toggle Full Auto / Semi Auto (Gun only)

### Tactical
- **C** - cycle crouch level (stand, crouch, low crouch, back to stand)
- **Q** - lean/peek left
- **E** - lean/peek right
- **X** - raise camera height slightly
- **Z** - lower camera height slightly

### Menu
- **Esc** - open/close pause menu
